About the Firm

Dedicated to Sound Legal Solutions in Cameroon

The Abeng Law Firm is a full-service law practice based in Douala, Cameroon, serving individuals, growing businesses and multinational clients. We combine deep knowledge of Cameroonian and OHADA law with a pragmatic, client-first approach to deliver clear, dependable legal guidance.

Our attorneys advise across corporate transactions, dispute resolution, real estate, employment and regulatory matters — supporting clients from initial strategy through to final resolution, in both French and English.
